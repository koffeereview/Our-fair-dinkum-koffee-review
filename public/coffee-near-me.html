How the Near Me Feature Works

01

Open the Koffee Review app and tap the 📍 Near Me button in the filter bar.

02

Allow location access when prompted. Your location is only used to sort the list — it is never stored or shared.

03

The café list instantly re-sorts showing every reviewed café within 4km of your current location, closest first with the distance shown on each card.

04

Tap any café to read the full review — score, tasting notes, map and Instagram review link.

Not Sure What to Order?

We order the same thing at every café — one latte and one double shot espresso. That is the only way to make every review directly comparable. If the café cannot make a good latte or a clean double shot, it does not score well. Simple as that.

A score of 7.5 or above earns the Koffee Review sticker. Anything below 3.9 goes on our avoid list. Everything in between is rated honestly with no agenda and no sponsorships.
