KOFFEE REVIEW

OUR STORY

We got tired of paying $5 for average coffee. So we started reviewing every cafe we visited. Same order, every time. No exceptions.

CHAPTER 01

THE PROBLEM

Brisbane has hundreds of cafes. Most of them serve average coffee. You walk in, pay $5 for a latte, take a sip, and think: "That was fine."

Fine is not good enough. Not when you are paying $5 to $7 for something that should be excellent. The problem is there is no way to know which cafes are actually good before you walk in. Google reviews are unreliable. Instagram is curated. Word of mouth is inconsistent.

We wanted a system. One that is honest, consistent, and impossible to manipulate.

CHAPTER 02

THE METHOD

We order one latte and one double shot espresso at every cafe. Same drinks. Same size. Every single time. No variations. No specials. No freebies.

"One latte. One double shot. Every time."

This is not negotiable. If a cafe offers us a free coffee, we decline. If a barista asks what we are reviewing for, we do not tell them. The score reflects what a regular customer would experience walking in off the street on any given day.

CHAPTER 05

THE BRAND

Our Fair Dinkum Koffee Review. Fair dinkum is Australian slang meaning genuine, honest, the real deal. That is the foundation of everything we do.

The K in Koffee is intentional. It makes the brand distinctive and recognisable. When you see the gold stamp, the dark background, and the score ring, you know it is a Koffee Review.

We are on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube as @koffeereview. Our website at koffeereview.com.au has every review, an interactive heat map, a cafe comparison tool, suburb guides, city rankings, and a spin the wheel feature for when you cannot decide where to go.

CHAPTER 06

THE MISSION

We want every person in Australia to know where to get great coffee before they walk in the door. No more gambling $5 on mediocre espresso. No more trusting Google reviews written by the cafe owner's friends.

"Know before you go."

That is what Koffee Review gives you. An honest score from someone who ordered the same thing you are about to order, paid the same price you are about to pay, and has nothing to gain by lying about it.

We are not food critics. We are not influencers. We are just two people who drink a lot of coffee and believe you deserve to know if it is worth your money.
